AeroE Checklist – Privacy policy

Guide

Privacy policy – version 2026-11-23

AeroE Checklist stores everything you enter on your own device: checklists and progress, aircraft and weight and balance profiles, passenger manifests, flight plans, flight records, the logbook, pilot details and, if you enter them, your NAIPS user ID and password and connector access token. Nothing is sent to the developer. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

What leaves the device, and only when you ask

  • NAIPS (Airservices Australia). When you request a briefing, charts, winds or file a flight plan, the installed app sends the request with your NAIPS login directly to Airservices, under Airservices' own terms and privacy policy. In a browser the request goes through a connector server (below).
  • Connector server. If you or your operator enter a connector address under Status › Operator link, briefings, winds, filing, chart requests and, when switched on, flight records and manifests are sent to that server. The server is chosen and run by you or your operator, not the developer; its operator is responsible for the data it holds.
  • Forecast winds. Load winds (forecast model) sends the route's leg positions and times to the Open-Meteo weather API to fetch winds and temperatures. No identity is sent.
  • Maps. Opening the map fetches map tiles from the map providers listed in the layers menu (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Esri, openAIP with your own key, RainViewer radar, or custom sources you add). Tile requests include the map area viewed.
  • Updates. The web app checks its own site for new versions. The installed app updates through TestFlight or the App Store.
  • Exports. Logbook, record, PDF and plan exports are files you create and share yourself.

Microphone and speech

Voice features, when you turn them on, use the device's speech recognition for short checklist commands. On iPad and iPhone the system recogniser may send audio to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the optional offline recogniser processes audio on the device. Audio is not recorded or kept by the app.

Your choices

Delete the app, or clear its site data in the browser, to remove everything it stored. Remove the NAIPS login and connector token under Status › Operator link at any time. Records uploaded to an operator's connector are the operator's to manage.

Children

The app is for licensed pilots and operators and is not directed at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated with the app; the version date above changes when it is. Material changes are noted in What's new.
